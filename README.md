# SWYNEX-Interactive-Dashboard
# 📊 Flipkart Mobile Brand Analysis Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** analyzing mobile phone performance, pricing, discounts, customer reviews, and brand market share on Flipkart.  
This project transforms raw e-commerce data into **actionable insights** for product strategy, customer engagement, and competitive positioning.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

With growing demands and cut‑throat competition in the smartphone market, this dashboard helps identify **which brands, products, and pricing strategies work best**.  
It provides executives, analysts, and business teams with a clear view of **sales trends, discount patterns, customer satisfaction, and brand performance**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Executive KPIs**

  - Product Performance

  - Price & Discount Analysis  
  - Brand Analysis  
  - Extra Info  

- **Core Visuals**
  - 📈 Avg Selling Price by Brand  
  - ⭐ Avg Rating by Brand  
  - 🏆 Top Brands by Number of Mobiles  
  - 🎯 Availability Status Distribution (In Stock, Coming Soon, Out of Stock)  
  - 📊 Market Share of Top 10 Brands  
  - 🔍 Rating vs Review Correlation

## 🛠️ Data Preparation

- **Data Cleaning (Power Query)**
  - Promoted headers & corrected data types  
  - Removed duplicates & standardized text fields  
  - Verified missing values and ensured consistency  

- **ETL Steps**
  - Split and reconstructed date columns for robust time intelligence  
  - Applied transformations for discount and pricing analysis  

---


This repository contains **five Power BI dashboards**, each focusing on a different aspect of analysis:

- `Product_Performance.pbix` – Product sales and performance metrics  
- `Customer_Review_Analysis.pbix` – Ratings, reviews, and sentiment insights  
- `Price_Discount_Analysis.pbix` – Pricing strategies and discount impact  
- `Brand_Analysis.pbix` – Market share and brand positioning  
- `Extra_Info.pbix` – Supporting data and additional insights  

👉 Place all five `.pbix` files inside a folder named **/dashboards** in this repository for easy access.


## 📈 Analytical Insights

- **Discount Trap** → Excessive discounts (>20%) erode profit margins.  
- **Category Profit Paradox** → High revenue categories (e.g., Furniture in Superstore dataset) often yield low net profitability.  
- **Geographic Imbalances** → Certain regions outperform others, requiring targeted strategies.  
- **Seasonality** → Q4 spikes in sales highlight the importance of inventory and staffing optimization.
